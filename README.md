## About

I'm a **Computer Science / AI student at Arab Open University** and a **freelance AI & software engineer**. I work across machine learning, computer vision, and full-stack web development with .NET and React.

I like understanding what a model is actually doing, so I work through the math behind algorithms with pen and paper before trusting them in code. Outside tech, you'll find me at the gym or watching films.

**Currently working on**
- 🩺 **SkinAi**: a full-stack AI medical web app (React + Python) for skin lesion analysis
- 💊 **Dawy**: an ASP.NET Core MVC platform for pharmacy management and medicine donation tracking

**Currently learning**
- 📐 Mathematical foundations of ML and NLP: Transformers, Reinforcement Learning, Anomaly Detection, Clustering
- 📄 Reading and implementing research papers

**Open to**: freelance projects, collaborations, and AI / software engineering opportunities.
